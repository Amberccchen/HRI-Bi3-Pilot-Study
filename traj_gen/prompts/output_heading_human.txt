Plan your walking as steps, one every {dt} s: [turn, distance]. turn is how many degrees your heading changes at the start of the step (positive turns clockwise on the map, from +u toward +v; negative turns anticlockwise). A turn is at most {max_turn} degrees either way, and a larger turn is cut to {max_turn}. distance is how far you then walk along your new heading, in {unit}, from 0 to the maximum per step. A distance of 0 means you wait in place for that step. A longer distance is cut to the maximum. The first step is for t + {dt} s, the second for t + {dt2} s, and so on. Give as many as needed to reach your goal, at most {max_steps}.
