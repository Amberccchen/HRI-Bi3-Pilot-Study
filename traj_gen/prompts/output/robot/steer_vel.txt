Plan the robot's motion as steps, one every {dt} s: [turn rate, speed], both held for the whole step. turn rate is how fast the robot's heading turns during the step, in degrees per second (positive turns {positive_turn} on the map, from +{c0} toward +{c1}; negative turns {negative_turn}), so it drives along a circular arc, or straight with a turn rate of 0, and the next step starts from the heading it ends with. A turn rate is at most {max_turn_rate} degrees per second either way, and a larger one is cut to {max_turn_rate}. speed is how fast it drives along the arc in that step, in {unit}/s, from 0 to the maximum speed. A speed of 0 means the robot waits in place for that step. A higher speed is cut to the maximum. The first step is for t + {dt} s, the second for t + {dt2} s, and so on.
