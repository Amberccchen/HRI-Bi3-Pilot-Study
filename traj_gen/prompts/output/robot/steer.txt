Plan the robot's motion as steps, one every {dt} s: [turn, distance]. turn is how many degrees the robot's heading changes at the start of the step (positive turns {positive_turn} on the map, from +{c0} toward +{c1}; negative turns {negative_turn}). A turn is at most {max_turn} degrees either way, and a larger turn is cut to {max_turn}. distance is how far it then moves along its new heading, in {unit}, from 0 to the maximum per step. A distance of 0 means the robot waits in place for that step. A longer distance is cut to the maximum. The first step is for t + {dt} s, the second for t + {dt2} s, and so on. Give as many as needed to reach the goal, at most {max_steps}.
