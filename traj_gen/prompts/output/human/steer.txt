Plan your walking as steps, one every {dt} s: [turn, distance]. turn is how many degrees your heading changes at the start of the step (positive turns {positive_turn} on the map, from +{c0} toward +{c1}; negative turns {negative_turn}). A turn is at most {max_turn} degrees either way, and a larger turn is cut to {max_turn}. distance is how far you then walk along your new heading in that step, in {unit}. A distance of 0 means you wait in place for that step. If a step is longer than your top speed allows, you walk only as far as it allows. The first step is for t + {dt} s, the second for t + {dt2} s, and so on. Give as many as needed to reach your goal, at most {max_steps}.
