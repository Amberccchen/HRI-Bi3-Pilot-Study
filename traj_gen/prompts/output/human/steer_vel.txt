Plan your walking as steps, one every {dt} s: [turn rate, speed], both held for the whole step. turn rate is how fast your heading turns during the step, in degrees per second (positive turns {positive_turn} on the map, from +{c0} toward +{c1}; negative turns {negative_turn}), so you walk along a circular arc, or straight with a turn rate of 0, and the next step starts from the heading you end with. A turn rate is at most {max_turn_rate} degrees per second either way, and a larger one is cut to {max_turn_rate}. speed is how fast you walk along the arc in that step, in {unit}/s. A speed of 0 means you wait in place for that step. If a speed is above your top speed, you walk at your top speed. The first step is for t + {dt} s, the second for t + {dt2} s, and so on. Give as many as needed to reach your goal, at most {max_steps}.
